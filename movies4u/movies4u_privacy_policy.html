Privacy Policy – Movies4u

Effective date: 22 November 2026

Movies4u (“the app”) is a free, ad-supported movie information app developed by Deepak Sharma. The app shows movie details, cast and crew information and official trailers. It does not host, stream or download any movies.

1. Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, phone number or email address. Your app preferences (such as the light or dark theme) are stored only on your device.

2. Third-party services

The app uses the following services, which may collect device information as described in their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob – shows ads and may use your device's advertising ID. Privacy policy
  • Google Analytics for Firebase – anonymous usage statistics. Privacy policy
  • Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Performance – crash reports and app performance data.
  • Firebase Remote Config – app settings.
  • TMDB (The Movie Database) – movie data and images. Privacy policy
  • YouTube – plays trailers. Privacy policy

3. Log data

If the app crashes, Firebase Crashlytics may collect data such as your device model, operating system version, app version and the time of the error. This is used only to fix problems.

4. Advertising

You can reset or opt out of personalised ads from your device settings: Settings > Google > Ads.

5. Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Links to other sites

The app may open third-party websites, such as YouTube. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes are posted on this page with a new effective date.

8. Contact us

If you have any questions, contact us at deepaksharmatheboss@gmail.com.
