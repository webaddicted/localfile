App name: Movies4u  |  Developer: Deepak Sharma  |  Effective date: 22 November 2026  |  Contact: deepaksharmatheboss@gmail.com

Movies4u (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is a free, ad-supported movie information application published by Deepak Sharma. By installing or using the app, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

The app helps you browse movie details, ratings, cast and crew information, posters, and official trailers. Movies4u does not host, stream, sell, rent, or let you download full-length movies or TV episodes. Any video you watch is an official trailer served through YouTube’s embedded player, not full copyrighted content from us.

1. Overview

Movies4u is an informational companion for cinema enthusiasts. We rely on third-party APIs and SDKs (including The Movie Database (TMDB), YouTube, Google Firebase, and Google AdMob) to deliver content, analytics, crash reporting, configuration, and advertising. Those partners may collect device and usage information under their own privacy policies.
